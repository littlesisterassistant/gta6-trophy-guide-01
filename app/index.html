Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours by PowerPyx's estimate (250+ by Push Square), difficulty 4 out of 10. No difficulty settings, no mission replay, no online trophies — and free roam continues after the story, so the cleanup can all be done at the end.

⚠ The one trophy that may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, one morning at Jason's house, Lucia says she is going to meditate and walks out to the balcony. Follow her and accept the optional quest — that meditation is gone after Chapter 1. PowerPyx reports it raises the meditation counter but is not marked as a spot like the other 18, so it is unclear whether the trophy needs it. Do it anyway: it takes a minute and removes the risk. (Source: PowerPyx, via sixshore.)

The 100% checklist, in brief

It opens after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. PowerPyx calls it lenient — closer to 60% true completion. You need 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations and 300 stunts; robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything; most side quests are not required, and collectibles are only partly required. Progress made during the story already counts. (Source: PowerPyx.)
